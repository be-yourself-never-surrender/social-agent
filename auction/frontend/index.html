100 tables per day, 5 days. Highest bid per table wins a 400,000 token lot and pays its own bid in full.

Round

Distributor claims

Winner tokens are held in one distributor contract per day and become claimable only when listing opens. Listing is currently closed in this prototype.

How it works

  • Each day offers 100 tables of 400,000 tokens. Each table is its own first price auction.
  • Next day opening bid equals the average of all closing prices from the previous day. Bids move in steps of 10 percent of the opening bid, rounded up.
  • One wallet may hold top bids on at most 2 tables at once. After 2 total wins, that wallet sits out the next 3 rounds, then returns with 1 table.
  • Bids are escrowed in USDT and cannot be cancelled. If you are outbid, claim your USDT at once or top up to retake the lead.
  • At each daily close, losers can claim USDT. Winners are recorded and their tokens are held by that day distributor until listing. No buyer protection: overbidding is the bidder's own fault.

Trust

  • Contract code will be public and verified. Status: TBD.
  • No claim function to the dev wallet exists in the distributor or liquidator contracts. Only winners can claim tokens. USDT only flows into the locked pool.
  • Liquidity pool tokens are burned permanently. No unlock date.
  • Operational gas for liquidity and contract transfers is paid from the dev personal wallet, never from user funds.
  • Post launch trading tax: buy 1 percent, sell 2 percent. Split: 50 percent dev, 30 percent buy back and burn, 20 percent treasury. Treasury use: TBD.
  • NFT trading fee: 2.5 percent from seller proceeds. Split: 70 percent token liquidity, 20 percent dev, 10 percent treasury.
